Act I

Why traditional commercial roofing sales does not work as well as it used to

Do you struggle getting in front of enough of the RIGHT building owners in your area? Do you find yourself wasting time with unqualified prospects or chasing dead-end leads? Do you wish you could close more commercial roof coating jobs FAST rather than spending months or years following up?

If you answered yes to any of those three questions, you are in good company. Most commercial roof coating contractors face these problems in their businesses. It is easy to blame “the industry,” “the economy,” or chalk it up to “that’s just how things have always been.”

But what if there was a better, faster, smarter way to grow your commercial roof coating business? What if you could generate 20–30 high-quality leads every month? What if those leads came to you pre-qualified and even educated on all the benefits of coatings?

This is all possible — and even simple — if you refuse to keep doing business the “old school” way.

Old-school sales

  • Flyers, door knocking, some SEO, some telemarketing.
  • The message: “Get A Free Roof Restoration Estimate!” and “Don’t Replace Your Roof, Restore It.”
  • Then inspect, and send a one- or two-page invoice-style bid.
  • It works. It also wastes months chasing people who were never ready.

The E.E.C. Method

  • Consultative sales plus education marketing, built for coatings.
  • Educate building owners in mass — 1,000:1, not 1:1.
  • Let them evaluate fit with a calculator before you roll a truck.
  • Calculate the 15-year savings so the job becomes a no-brainer.

The E.E.C. Method combines the time-tested approach of consultative sales and education marketing into a framework for growing your roof coating business. It is engineered to help commercial roof coating contractors get more qualified leads and close them with ease. Below is the process. Take it and apply it.

Act II

The E.E.C. Method: educate, then evaluate, then calculate

1

Educate

How to generate high-quality roof coating leads with education marketing.

When you are selling a high-ticket commercial roofing solution, the prospect has to weigh a lot of factors. The more educated and qualified they are before you meet, the higher the likelihood you close.

Restoring or replacing a commercial roof is expensive and complicated. That expense and complexity is why owners put the decision off and go cold. We want to avoid that at all costs.

Using education marketing, you let leads come to you educated, pre-qualified, and ready to make a decision. You assume the role of the authority and trusted advisor. You are not just any old roofing contractor who wants the job and the money. You are the expert who can teach the building owner and look out for his best interest.

How it works

If you have ever explained the benefits of roof coating on the phone or on a roof, you have already done education marketing — one person at a time. The E.E.C. Method takes that same knowledge, turns it into an article, e-book, or video, and lets it work 1,000:1.

A simple, effective start is a blog post titled “10 Things [STATE] Building Owners Must Know About Roof Coating.” List the ten things every owner should know. It captures attention because it is not selling anything. Not a writer? Have someone record you on a phone camera while you explain coatings, roof types, and the questions owners actually ask. Or hire a writer to interview you and turn the transcript into posts.

The goal is for the owner to think, “Wow, this contractor is a true expert!” That is what creates trust. Then get the content in front of the right prospects: Facebook, a mailed printout, LinkedIn to property managers. Direct “free estimate” pitches rarely work on social. Education does, because it is value, not a sales shove.

At any given time, only about 3% of a market is ready to buy now. Another ~27% will buy in the next 6 to 24 months. Education marketing lets you tap that larger pool. And you only need to do it once: 1 to 3 well-written articles or videos can cover 90% of the information and benefits. It is a set-it-and-forget-it approach.

2

Evaluate

How to pre-qualify leads with a free online evaluation tool.

After you have educated the prospect, you have earned their attention. Some will call for an estimate. Many more will not be there yet. That is when you let them evaluate whether roof coating is right for them — with a free cost-estimate calculator that prices a coating from the answers the building owner provides.

The benefits

This gives the building owner more value without you doing a thing. It filters out people who only want a number and have no plan to restore the roof. Leads from a calculator are higher quality because they already know the approximate cost — otherwise they will not ask you to come out. Again, the key is leverage: educate and evaluate in mass. You can only speak to so many owners in a day. A blog post and a calculator can serve thousands.

3

Calculate

How to close more deals faster by calculating the savings with the prospect.

By educating the building owner, evaluating their situation, and then calculating the project’s long-term savings, you walk them to a no-brainer. Commercial roofers typically do not take the time to break this down. Roof coating is much cheaper than replacement — they know that. What they often do not see is that a coating can also help lower utility bills by up to 25%.

Act III

Show them the money they keep for 15 years

Wouldn’t it make sense to show the building owner how much money he could save over 15 years with a white reflective roof?

$2,000 monthly electric bill
15 years $360,000 spent on power
$72,000 saved at just 20%

If he saves just 20% on electricity, that is more than $72,000 over 15 years. With an SPF system, the savings can be even greater — the roof can pay for itself in as little as 7 years in some cases.

Do not stop there. A coating can be recoated at the end of its lifecycle. Another coating is much cheaper than another replacement. That is a second savings conversation worth having.

Then there is the immediate tax deduction via IRS Section 179, which can let the building owner deduct the full expense of the roof coating that year. In our experience, this is a huge incentive.

Educate. Evaluate. Calculate. You are no longer sending a thin bid into a cold inbox. You are walking a qualified owner to a decision that already makes financial sense.
